Legal & Privacy

License and Privacy Information

Here is a plain-language summary of the January 14, 2026 WonderWedding EULA and Privacy Policy. For exact legal wording, download the official PDF.

What the legal document says

Updated Jan 14, 2026

Location data disclosures

WonderWedding may collect background location data to enable location-based planning features, nearby discovery, and geofenced reminders, even when the app is not actively in use. You can revoke this permission anytime in device settings.

License scope and limits

You get a personal, non-commercial, revocable license. The agreement blocks reverse engineering, resale, scraping, malware use, and unauthorized system access.

Account responsibilities

You must provide accurate registration details, protect your credentials, and report unauthorized access quickly. WonderWedding can suspend accounts for violations.

Who can use the app

Accounts are for users 18+, or younger users with parent/guardian permission where local law allows.

What data may be collected

The policy lists account details, device and usage logs, location data (with consent), AI prompts and responses, and payment transaction metadata via third-party processors.

How data is used and shared

Data is used to run the app, personalize experiences, process payments, improve security, and comply with law. Sharing may occur with providers, partners (with consent), legal authorities, or in business transfers.

Your privacy rights

The policy includes rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and complaint filing with a data authority.

More legal highlights

AI transparency and limits

The app discloses Google Gemini integration, warns that AI outputs can be inaccurate or biased, and states AI content is not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Intellectual property

App code, content, and marks remain company property. Feedback can be used by the company without compensation obligations.

Liability, disputes, and termination

The agreement limits company liability, requires user indemnification in some claim scenarios, uses binding arbitration for many disputes, and defines what survives termination.

Contact and rights requests

Privacy and legal requests can be sent to support@WonderWedding.org, and users may request human review for significant automated decisions.
